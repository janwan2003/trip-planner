# /brag plan: WeGoWhen, social cut (vertical)

**Brief from the owner:** made for Instagram Reels and TikTok, something people would
want to watch and send on, not a commercial.

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, ~17.5s. Captions sit in the upper third, clear of
the platform UI (right-hand buttons, bottom caption bar).

## Angle: a skit everyone has lived through

The meme shape "me after saying *we should do a trip in June*". A group chat melts down
for nine days. Then one person drops a single link, the friends tap their days, and the
chat's last word is "booking flights". The product never gets a pitch. It turns up as the
punchline, the way you'd tag the friend who always ends up organising.

Why it's shareable: the chat is the relatable part and people tag their own group chat.
The product appears for about six seconds, as a screen recording on a phone. The CTA is
a message in the chat ("wegowhen.com"), not an end card.

**Real UI:** the invitation link's actual unfurl (title and description from
`functions/trip/[id].ts` on the local build, `og-image.png`), and a real screen recording
of the mobile trip page at 390px: a friend dragging across his days, the heat map filling
in, Best Dates answering the range all six can make. All filmed from `wrangler pages dev`
with a throwaway local D1 and made-up names.

The chat itself is illustrative (made-up friends, generic dark-mode messenger, not any
real app's branding). No invented product claims, numbers or testimonials.
