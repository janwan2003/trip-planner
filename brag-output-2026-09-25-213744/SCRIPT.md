# Make it yours: two minutes with your phone

The video is a real screen recording, but what makes this kind of post work is a real
person. There are two easy ways to add yourself. Both use `brag-no-music.mp4`.

## Option A: voiceover (easiest)

Open `brag-no-music.mp4` in TikTok, Instagram or CapCut and record a voiceover. Talk the
way you'd explain it to a friend, not like you're reading. Rough lines, timed to the video:

- **0s:** "Every time my friends try to plan a trip, the group chat just dies. So I built a thing."
- **5s:** "You make a trip, give it a name, pick roughly when… (typo, whatever)"
- **14s:** "Copy the link, throw it in the group chat."
- **18s:** "Everyone just puts their name in and drags over the days they're free. No app, no account."
- **29s:** "Then a few days later you open it and…"
- **31s:** "…it just tells you which days everyone can actually go."
- **35s:** "It's free. wegowhen.com."

Keep the typed captions on. Most people watch without sound.

## Option B: green screen (best for reach)

Use TikTok's or Instagram's green screen effect with `brag-no-music.mp4` as the
background, and film yourself in the bottom corner saying the same lines. A face in the
first second is the strongest hook these feeds have. Film in your normal voice, in a
normal room. One take is fine, and the slight mess is the point.
